Evidence of practice

Knowledge makes you aware (1); only shipped, reviewed work makes you practiced (2). Check only what you have actually done — gates are verified by reviewers in the cohort, and unverifiable claims collapse the whole matrix. "I could do it" is not "I did it."

Teaching attestation → can-teach (3)

Check a discipline only if you have actually taught it — a cohort module, a workshop, structured pairing. Rating 3 requires rating 2 evidence too.
